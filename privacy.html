Privacy

Your data stays with you.

FandomVerse is a student project. Almost everything you do here is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Stored on your device

Your account, cart, orders and bookmarks are kept in this browser, not on a server.

No plain passwords

Passwords are turned into a one-way SHA-256 hash. The real password is never saved.

No tracking or ads

No analytics, no advertising cookies, no selling of data.

No real payments

Checkout is a demo. No card is charged and nothing is shipped.

What we store, and where

DataWhereHow long
Account (name, email, password hash)This browser (local storage)Until you clear site data
Login sessionThis tab (session storage)Until you close the tab or log out
Cart and ordersThis browser, per accountUntil you clear site data
Bookmarks and notesThis browserUntil you remove them
Profile picture, cover, theme colourThis browser, per accountUntil you change or clear them
Chat conversationThis tabUntil you close the tab
Accessibility settingsThis browserUntil you reset them

None of this is uploaded to a FandomVerse database. On another browser or device, it won't be there.

The AI assistant

When you ask the Guide something, your conversation is sent through our small server to an AI service (Groq, OpenRouter or Hugging Face) so it can write an answer. Only the chat text is sent: not your account, cart or password. The API keys stay on the server and are never shown to your browser. Please don't type personal or sensitive information into the chat.

Voice and microphone

The microphone is used only while you press the mic button in the chat, and your browser asks for permission first. Speech is turned into text by the browser's speech recognition (in Chrome and Edge this uses Google's service). Character voices are audio files played from this site.

Videos and 3D models

Videos that aren't stored on the site play from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which avoids setting tracking cookies until you play. 3D models, images and stored clips load from this site. Creators and licences are listed on the About page and each character page.

Questions

Can anyone else see my orders or cart?

No. They're stored only in your browser, separately for each account.

What happens if I clear my browser?

Your FandomVerse account and everything saved with it are removed from that browser. You can sign up again at any time.

Is my password safe?

We never store it. We keep a one-way hash, so even the site can't read your password.

Does the chat remember me?

Only for the current tab, so you can move between pages. Close the tab and it's gone.

Who do I contact about privacy?

Use the Contact page.
