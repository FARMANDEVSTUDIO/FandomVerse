Who we are

Built by fans, for fans

FandomVerse was created for the TechWiz7 — Web Innovation Unleashed competition under the theme Fandom Universe. Fans usually jump between dozens of sites to follow their favourite shows, games and groups. FandomVerse brings seven of them together in one fast, friendly hub.

Our mission

Make every fandom easy to explore

Whether you're waiting for the next Jujutsu Kaisen episode, planning a PUBG squad night, counting down to a BTS comeback or re-reading One Piece, you'll find the stories, characters, events and videos for it here, and a guide to help you find them.

What makes it different

More than a fan wiki

Characters in 3D

Nine characters are real 3D models: drag to turn them all the way round, switch views, zoom out, even change Iron Man's armour.

They speak for themselves

Press Play voice and the character introduces itself in a recorded voice, with every word written on screen as it's said.

An AI guide on every page

Ask anything about the site in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. It knows every character, event, price and page.

Talk to it, it acts

Tap the mic and say "Goku ka page kholo" or "play the Batman trailer" and the site does it for you.

Official videos

Trailers, interviews and behind-the-scenes clips from the studios themselves, 2 to 3 minutes each, playable right on the page.

Your data stays yours

Accounts, carts, orders and bookmarks live in your own browser. Passwords are only ever stored as a secure hash.

How we built it

The journey

  1. 01

    Plan the universe

    Seven fandoms, one design system: a glass-style dark theme, category colours and a shared card layout.

  2. 02

    Fill it with content

    Articles, 33 character profiles, 21 events, 15 releases, merch and official videos, all kept in simple JSON files.

  3. 03

    Bring characters to life

    Background-removed turnarounds, then real 3D models (compressed from up to 345 MB to a few MB) and recorded voices with word-by-word timing.

  4. 04

    Add a brain

    An AI guide with voice input that knows the whole site, works in three languages and can carry out commands.

  5. 05

    Polish for everyone

    Responsive layouts, privacy by design and an accessibility panel so every fan can use it comfortably.
