Accessibility

Built for every fan.

Use FandomVerse the way that suits you: bigger text, stronger contrast, less motion, the keyboard, a screen reader or your voice.

What we've built in

Accessible by design

Keyboard friendly

Every link, button, card, filter and dialog works with Tab, Enter and Space, with a clear focus outline.

Screen readers

Real headings and landmarks, labelled icons, alt text on images and announced updates.

Contrast & themes

Readable contrast in both dark and light mode, plus the high-contrast setting above.

Respects motion settings

If your device asks for less motion, animations are reduced automatically.

Any screen, any zoom

Works on phones, tablets and desktops and stays usable at 200% zoom.

Words on screen

When a character speaks, every word appears on screen as it's said.

Voice control

Speak to the guide in English, Hinglish, Urdu or Hindi and it can open pages for you.

Ask for help

The guide explains any feature in plain words, in your language.

Standards

Our aim

We follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA as closely as we can.

  • Text alternatives for images
  • Keyboard access and visible focus
  • Readable contrast in both themes
  • Resizable text up to 200%
  • Motion can be reduced
  • Captions-style words for character voices

Known limits: 3D models are visual (the same information is on the page as text); voice input needs Chrome or Edge and internet; YouTube videos use YouTube's own player and captions.

Found something hard to use? Tell us and we'll fix it.
